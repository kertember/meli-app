Füzet for iPhone

A preview at iPhone 16 Pro size (402 × 874 points). The students are samples. The clock is fixed at Tuesday 6 October, 10:40, so the earlier hours show as past.

Try it

  • Tap an empty hour to add a student.
  • In Nap, swipe a student left and tap the pen to change them, or swipe right and tap the bin to delete them.
  • In Hét, tap a student to change them.
  • Messages such as “Elmentve” disappear after 1.5 seconds. Swipe one down to close it sooner.
  • Tap the date to open the calendar.
  • Use ‹ › to move a day or a week. In Hét you can also swipe sideways.
  • Switch between Nap and Hét at the bottom. In Hét, tap a weekday to open that day.
